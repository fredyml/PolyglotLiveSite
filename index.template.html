Core Capabilities

Two directions. One seamless conversation.

Instead of reading delayed subtitles or typing into translation boxes, Polyglot Live works directly with your computer's audio to translate spoken voice naturally.

1. Your voice translated live

Speak naturally into your regular microphone. Polyglot Live translates and synthesizes your speech in the chosen destination language for other participants in real time.

2. Incoming speech translated to you

When the other participant answers in their native language, the incoming audio is translated on the fly and played directly through your headphones.

3. Works with any meeting platform

Compatible with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, Skype, or browser-based calls. No special browser extensions or third-party add-ons needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Can I purchase a plan on this website? +

No, this website is purely informative. All plan purchases, subscriptions, and on-demand time packs are handled directly inside the Polyglot Live Windows desktop application once you sign in with your Google or personal Microsoft account.

What does Polyglot Live do? +

Polyglot Live is a Windows desktop application that translates spoken voice bidirectionally in real time. It translates what you say into your interlocutor's language, and translates what they reply back into your native language during live calls on Zoom, Teams, Meet, or Discord.

Does it work with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and Discord? +

Yes. Polyglot Live coordinates directly with Windows audio endpoints. It captures your microphone and delivers translated audio through your normal speaker or headphone output without requiring browser extensions or extra plugins.

How long is my purchased balance valid? +

Purchased packages—both monthly subscriptions and one-time flexible passes—are valid for a 30-day billing cycle. There is no daily cap, meaning you can consume your hours at any time within your active 30-day period.

How does sign-in work? +

Simply click the provider button (Google or personal Microsoft accounts such as Outlook or Hotmail). Your default browser handles authentication directly with the provider, and you return to Polyglot Live ready to translate.

Who develops and supports this software? +

Polyglot Live is an independent software application created and maintained by Fredy Mendoza. There are no corporate intermediaries: you receive focused Windows software, direct communication, and transparent pricing.

What are the system requirements? +

Windows 10 (version 1703 or later) or Windows 11, a reliable internet connection for live translation processing, a functioning microphone, and headphones or speakers.
