Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

This Privacy Policy describes how Polyglot Live, developed and operated by Fredy Mendoza (Official contact: fredyml2402@gmail.com), processes information when you use the Polyglot Live Windows desktop application and this website.

1. Voice Audio in Transit (Zero Cloud Recording)

Audio captured during a live translation session is streamed in real time over encrypted connections (TLS/WSS) solely to generate simultaneous voice translation and live captions. Polyglot Live never records, stores, or retains audio files of your conversations on its servers.

2. Local Text Transcripts (Automatic 3-Day Deletion)

Synchronized text transcripts generated during a session are stored exclusively on your local Windows computer disk and are automatically purged after 3 days (72 hours). Transcript files are never uploaded to Polyglot Live servers.

3. Google & Microsoft OAuth 2.0 Sign-In Data

Polyglot Live uses official OAuth 2.0 with PKCE via your default web browser to authenticate your account with Google or Microsoft. We only receive your basic account identifier, email address, and display name strictly to associate your account with your purchased translation plan and remaining time quota. Polyglot Live does not access your emails, contacts, calendars, or cloud files, and never sells, rents, or shares Google or Microsoft user data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes, in full compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy (including Limited Use requirements).

4. Payment Processing via Mercado Pago

All plan purchases and subscriptions are processed in your web browser through Mercado Pago. Polyglot Live never collects, processes, or stores credit/debit card numbers, bank account credentials, PIX keys, PSE credentials, or SPEI details. We only receive payment confirmation metadata, plan status, and quota allocation.

5. International Data Transfers & Sub-processors

To deliver the service, strictly necessary data is transmitted securely over encrypted channels to: Google / Microsoft (OAuth authentication), Mercado Pago (checkout and subscription billing across Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Mexico, Peru, Uruguay, and other supported countries), and Google Cloud / Gemini Live (real-time in-transit voice interpretation).

6. Your Privacy Rights (ARCO / Habeas Data / LGPD / GDPR)

You may exercise your rights to access, rectify, update, oppose, revoke consent, or request deletion of your account at any time by contacting the Data Controller directly: Fredy Mendoza — fredyml2402@gmail.com.

2. Transcripciones 100 % locales con borrado automático a los 3 días

Las transcripciones de texto se guardan exclusivamente en el almacenamiento local del equipo Windows del usuario y se eliminan automáticamente a las 72 horas (3 días). Nunca se envían a los servidores de Polyglot Live.
